Privacy

Image Studio by AGS Labs · Updated 15 October 2026

Image Studio runs on your PC. We do not collect anything: no account, no analytics, no tracking. Your prompts and pictures go somewhere else only when you connect a cloud provider and use it.

What stays on your PC

  • Pictures and prompts. Every picture is saved in your library folder (by default Pictures\Image Studio) with the prompt and settings that made it.
  • Settings and what the app learns from you. Kept in your user folders (%APPDATA%\Image Studio and %LOCALAPPDATA%\Image Studio).
  • API keys. Keys you enter for cloud providers are stored on your PC only, encrypted by Windows for your user account, so they cannot be read on another account or computer.
  • Models. Models you install run on your own graphics card. Pictures made with them never leave your PC.

When something leaves your PC

  • Cloud providers you connect. If you add a key for a provider such as OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic, fal.ai, Replicate or OpenRouter and choose it, the prompt and any picture you are editing are sent to that provider to make the result. Their privacy policy covers what they do with it. Local servers such as Ollama or LM Studio stay on your PC or network.
  • Downloads. Installing the engine or a model downloads it from its source (PyTorch, the Python Package Index, Hugging Face). Those sites see your IP address, as with any download. A Hugging Face token, if a model needs one, is used for that download and not stored. The app turns off Hugging Face's usage statistics.
  • Reports. "Report a problem" and "Report this picture" open a GitHub issue in your browser, filled in with the app's version and, for a picture, its prompt and model. Nothing is sent until you submit it there, and you can change or remove anything first. GitHub issues are public.

This website

agslabs.tech is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) to run the site. The page asks GitHub for the newest release so the download buttons point to it, and your browser keeps that answer until you close the tab. We use no cookies and no analytics.

Your control

  • Delete any picture, or the whole library folder, at any time.
  • Remove a provider key in Settings → Providers.
  • Remove the engine in Settings → Models. To remove everything, uninstall the app and delete the folders above and your models folder (shown in Settings → This PC).

Children

Image Studio is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/gaffar273/imageStudio. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
